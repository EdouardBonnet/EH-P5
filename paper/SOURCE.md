This is the LaTeX source of *Induced subgraph density. VII. The five-vertex path* by Tung Nguyen, Alex Scott, and Paul Seymour, [arXiv:2312.15333v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.15333v3).

Downloaded from https://arxiv.org/src/2312.15333v3. The paper is distributed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Its text, authorship, statements, and proofs are unchanged. The explicit `pdftex` option of `hyperref` is removed so that the package can select the driver for both the PDF and web compilation engines. Added `% lax begin` / `% lax end` comments connect passages to the formalization.

The formal statements clear density denominators and sometimes enlarge constants. The concept cards describe these choices. The final Lean proof uses a critical-graph argument from the house dichotomy; the paper instead proves the stronger polynomial Rödl property. No annotation identifies these two proof arguments as the same.
